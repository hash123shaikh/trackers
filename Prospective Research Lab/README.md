## 🌐 EngerLab – Physics & Engineering in Medicine

**Website**: [EngerLab at McGill](https://www.mcgill.ca/medphys/research/engerlab)  
**PI**: Dr. Shirin Abbasinejad Enger  
Associate Professor, Medical Physics Unit, Gerald Bronfman Department of Oncology, McGill University  
Director, Medical Physics Unit  
Tier 2 Canada Research Chair in Medical Physics

### 👥 Who We Are:
A multidisciplinary group of researchers and trainees in medical physics, radiation biology, AI, software engineering, and more, working toward developing personalized cancer treatment technologies.

### 💡 What We Do:
The EngerLab focuses on enabling patient-specific radiotherapy treatments by:
- Studying radiation interaction with matter
- Creating novel sources, delivery systems, and detectors
- Developing Monte Carlo and AI-based dose optimization
- Building outcome prediction models using multimodal data

These innovations aim to improve treatment response, reduce side effects, and enhance patient quality of life.

### 🔬 Research Groups:
- **Artificial Intelligence**: Deep learning models for patient-specific brachytherapy  
- **Radiobiology & Microdosimetry**: Studies on RBE differences between radiation sources  
- **Detector Development**: Clinical-grade radiation detectors  
- **Novel Brachytherapy Technology**: Monte Carlo-based TPS, 90Y radioembolization, IMBT systems

### 🧑‍🔬 About the PI:
Dr. Enger earned her PhD at Uppsala University and completed her postdoc at Université Laval. She leads major national and international committees, and her lab has won numerous innovation awards. She currently leads projects in:
1. Brachytherapy technology  
2. Radiobiology and microdosimetry  
3. Detector development  
4. AI-based clinical solutions
