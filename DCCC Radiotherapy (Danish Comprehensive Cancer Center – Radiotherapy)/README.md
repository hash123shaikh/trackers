# 🇩🇰 DCCC Radiotherapy Tracker

DCCC Radiotherapy (Danish Comprehensive Cancer Center – Radiotherapy) is a national research infrastructure established with support from the **Knæk Cancer funds** and led by coordinators like **Prof. Stine Korreman**.

🔗 Official website: [straaleterapi.dk/om-os](https://www.straaleterapi.dk/om-os/)  
🔗 DESIRE project: [straaleterapi.dk/desire](https://www.straaleterapi.dk/projekter/desire/)

This folder tracks:
- Research workgroups and clinical groups
- DCCC-affiliated PhD projects (past and ongoing)
- Strategic infrastructure projects like **DESIRE**
- Prospective labs and supervisors in Denmark
