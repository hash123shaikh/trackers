# 💻 DESIRE – Data Science Infrastructure for Radiotherapy

🔗 [Official Description](https://www.straaleterapi.dk/projekter/desire/)  
Funded by: Novo Nordisk Foundation (2023–2028)

## 🔍 Objective
To create a unified national infrastructure for:
- Image and dose plan standardization
- Data collection across Danish RT centers
- Support AI-driven automation in:
  - Segmentation
  - Dose planning
  - QA
  - Outcome prediction

## 💡 Key Themes
- Radiotherapy big data
- Interoperability across departments
- Deep learning and model-based RT
- Clinical and research synergy

This project ties in with:
- DCCC clinical groups
- PhD projects involving imaging, AI, and outcome modeling
- Supervisors like **Prof. Stine Korreman**, a collaborator
